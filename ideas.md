# EventSponsor design direction

## Direction shortlist
- **Signal & Soil** — grounded Ugandan marketplace with warm clay, ink, and growth green. Probability 0.06.
- **Night Market** — dark editorial dashboard with electric lime data accents. Probability 0.04.
- **Sunlit Ledger** — bright civic-tech system with amber highlights and blue trust markers. Probability 0.03.

## Committed direction: Signal & Soil
EventSponsor should feel like a trusted local business network: editorial warmth for the marketplace, crisp information hierarchy for decisions, and energetic data moments for match scores and campaign results.

- **Design movement:** contemporary African civic-tech with a boutique marketplace sensibility.
- **Core principles:** trust before hype, evidence over vague promises, warm human context around structured decisions.
- **Color philosophy:** deep ink/navy for confidence, parchment for breathing room, vivid leaf green for verified actions, terracotta for energetic highlights, amber for attention states.
- **Layout paradigm:** wide editorial hero, dense dashboard cards, asymmetric split panels, rounded but not bubbly surfaces, generous gutters.
- **Signature elements:** circular match gauges, verification chips, Kampala location pins, small metric labels, campaign timeline steps.
- **Interaction philosophy:** direct, low-friction, visibly reversible demo interactions with concise toast feedback.
- **Animation:** restrained slide/fade transitions and animated progress bars; never rely on motion to communicate meaning.
- **Typography:** Manrope for display and body, Space Mono for compact numeric labels.
- **Brand essence:** make sponsorship feel measurable, local, and possible.
- **Brand voice:** clear, credible, optimistic, commercially useful.
- **Wordmark/logo:** a simple linked loop / handshake mark formed by two opposing arcs, paired with the EventSponsor wordmark.
- **Signature brand color:** Signal Green `#B7F36B` against Ink `#102420`.
